The questioner completes one cover sheet per pull request and attaches copies
of \texttt{qa\_sup.pdf} to record questions and evaluations.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.15em,topsep=0.4em]
  \item Print and complete by hand in ink.
  \item Conduct the QA live on a real-time meeting platform. The PR author
        must answer live without AI assistance and must not complete this record.
  \item The questioner must be the next technical authority as defined in
        GOVERNANCE.md.
  \item Keep the original for four months after merge. The Project Maintainer
        may request delivery within the first three months; the Hotaru
        maintenance team covers the fee. Failure to produce a requested
        original may be referred for governance review.
  \item Write in English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, or Simplified Chinese.
\end{enumerate}
